# Product: Croatian Working Law Fact Checker

## Summary
A multilingual search tool for the Croatian labor law (Zakon o radu). Foreign workers in Croatia can search articles in Croatian, English, or Spanish and read verified excerpts with relevance scoring.

## Audience
Foreign workers in Croatia who need to understand their labor rights. They search in their own language and need clear, trustworthy answers with article references.

## Jobs to be done
- Find the relevant article for a question (vacation, working hours, termination, sick leave).
- Read the verified excerpt in their language.
- Understand which article number and section applies.
- Trust the answer (cite the official source).

## Success
A user finds the right article in under 30 seconds and understands the key point without reading legalese.

## Constraints
- Static site (GitHub Pages), no backend.
- Multilingual (hr/en/es).
- Data sourced from official Croatian labor law documents.
- Must remain accessible and readable on mobile.

## Design direction
Replace neomorphic soft-UI with a flat, modern, high-contrast interface: clean typography, clear hierarchy, article cards with badges and highlighted key terms, tooltips for article references.
